Benchmark runner

Regenerates the data behind the chart on the main page. Each method runs alone (so they don't compete for CPU) on a ladder of n, until the next step would take more than 40 s or 1.5 GB. Fast inputs are timed over many repetitions and the median of several runs is kept. Each mode is measured on its own and the other mode's data is kept as it is. This takes several minutes; paste the output into js/bench-data.js.
